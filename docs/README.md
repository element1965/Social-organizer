Social Organizer is the open-source utility of EXODUS 2.0, a digital societal innovation (DSI). Theory and related material:

- Andrei Lubalin, [*From Human Behaviour to Social Coordination: Report on a Completed Theoretical and Engineering Research Programme and Its End-User Implementation*](https://zenodo.org/records/21629935), report, Zenodo, 2026, doi 10.5281/zenodo.21629935; mirrored on the [P2P Foundation wiki](https://wiki.p2pfoundation.net/From_Human_Behaviour_to_Social_Coordination).
- Andrei Lubalin, [*Discovery of the Law of Autocatalytic Inevitability: A New Natural Law Governing Network Self-Organization*](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15778286), preprint, Zenodo, 2025, doi 10.5281/zenodo.15778286.

## Project writing skills

The project's Claude Code skills (how the project is told, explained and reviewed) are kept by the maintainers outside the repository.

| Skill | Use |
|---|---|
| `so-story` | Explain the project in landing copy, posts, onboarding, FAQ and assistant responses |
| `so-trust-lens` | Review changes against the three trust conditions: repeat interactions, mutual benefit and clear communication |
| `so-explainer` | Teach by play and prediction, including the landing explainer and `/how` |
