# The core message (draft for the team)

> Summary: Stage 2 of the packaging plan — the headline, the one sentence, the story in five beats, the answer to "why join now", the questions a newcomer still asks, three proof points, what SO is not, one line and one ask per audience, and how to test it (EN and RU). Draft, revised after a cold read, waiting for the team.

## Context

Out of the whole pool, these are the few lines that every landing, video, onboarding screen,
influencer kit and donor letter starts from. Sources: [what SO is](what-so-is.md),
[principles](../principles/principles.md), [claims](../principles/claims.md),
[the Need protocol](../mechanics/need-protocol.md), [latent connectedness](../theory/latent-connectedness.md),
[audiences](../audiences/audiences.md), [landing concepts](../packaging/landing-concepts.md).
Written with the `so-story` skill, then revised after a cold read (section 11).

**Status: draft of 2026-11-18, waiting for the team** (the user and Andrei Lubalin). Once accepted,
the canonical lines in [what SO is](what-so-is.md) follow this note.

## Details

### 1. The headline

| | EN | RU |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | The world is already connected. | Мир уже связан. |
| Second line | Now the people you know can share each other's risks. | Теперь люди, которых вы знаете, могут делить риски друг друга. |
| Supporting fact | Two people on Facebook are on average fewer than four handshakes apart. | Между двумя пользователями Facebook в среднем меньше четырёх рукопожатий. |

Why this one:
- It is the project's research premise (3.57 handshakes in 2016, Edunov et al.) and it surprises,
  so it works as the opening puzzle in Case's method.
- It puts the network first, as the inspirer asked, and the second line gives the reason to care.
- It sets SO apart at once: donation sites, insurers and savings groups all start from money.

The risk is abstraction: a cold reader called the headline "vague marketing" on its own. The
supporting fact and the first scene must make it concrete within seconds. "Share each other's
risks" is the agreed public wording; the cold reader heard insurance in it, so the five-person test
(section 10) checks it. The current landing hero, "First, a network of people you know", becomes the
heading of the first step.

### 2. The one sentence

**EN.** When one of you has a hard time, you report a Need, and it reaches the people you know and the
people they know: up to 300 of them for $300, nearest first. Each of them decides whether to take a
small part of the amount.

**RU.** Когда кому-то из вас трудно, он сообщает о потребности, и её получают люди, которых вы знаете,
и их знакомые: до 300 человек на $300, сначала ближайшие. Каждый сам решает, взять ли небольшую
часть суммы.

### 3. The story in five beats

The same spine for the landing, a video, onboarding and an influencer's post.

| # | EN | RU |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **You confirm the people you know.** Each of them confirms you, and through them you reach the people they know. | **Вы подтверждаете тех, кого знаете лично.** Они подтверждают вас, и через них вы связаны с их знакомыми. |
| 2 | **A Need reaches one person for every dollar.** When one of you has a hard time, they report a Need: an amount and a chat link. A $300 Need reaches up to 300 people, nearest first. | **Потребность получают по человеку на каждый доллар.** Когда кому-то трудно, он сообщает о потребности: сумма и ссылка на чат. Потребность на $300 получают до 300 человек, сначала ближайшие. |
| 3 | **Each person finds out and decides.** You see the chain of people between you and the author, and you can ask the one you know. Nobody has to answer. | **Каждый выясняет и решает сам.** Вы видите цепочку людей между вами и автором и можете спросить того, кого знаете. Отвечать никто не обязан. |
| 4 | **Small amounts from many people add up.** Those who want report Participation: they say how much they are ready to give, from $1, and can change it while the Need is open. Nobody pays through the app. | **Небольшие суммы многих людей складываются.** Желающие сообщают об участии: называют, сколько готовы дать, от $1, и могут изменить это, пока потребность открыта. Через приложение никто не платит. |
| 5 | **People agree directly.** They talk in their own chat. If they agree on money, they hand it over to each other, not through the app. | **Люди договариваются напрямую.** Они общаются в своём чате. Если речь о деньгах, люди передают их друг другу сами, не через приложение. |
| → | **Next time, the one who asks may be you.** | **В следующий раз попросить можете вы.** |

### 4. Why join now

The question every newcomer asks: "Why join, when I don't need anything?"

| | EN | RU |
|---|---|---|
| Answer | The network has to exist before the bad day. | Сеть нужна до трудного дня, а не в него. |
| Reason 1 | It costs nothing: joining is free and the app takes no fee. Taking part is always your choice. | Это ничего не стоит: вход бесплатный, приложение не берёт комиссий. Участвовать или нет, вы решаете каждый раз сами. |
| Reason 2 | You start with people you already know, not strangers. | Вы начинаете с тех, кого уже знаете, а не с незнакомцев. |
| Reason 3 | Nobody earns from inviting. You add people because a network of people you know is worth having. | Никто не зарабатывает на приглашениях. Вы добавляете людей, потому что сеть знакомых стоит того, чтобы она была. |

The access rule (five people you know and ten in your network before your first Need) is explained
where people meet it, in the app, not in this message: a cold reader read it as recruiting.

### 5. The questions a newcomer still asks

| Question | EN answer | RU answer |
|---|---|---|
| Who makes sure people keep their word? | Nobody in the middle. You decide whom to trust through people you know, and you talk to them directly. | Никто посередине. Вы решаете, кому доверять, через знакомых, и говорите с людьми напрямую. |
| Does the app take a cut? | No. There is no fee and no commission; the code is open and the project is non-profit. | Нет. Никаких комиссий; код открыт, проект некоммерческий. |
| Is my Participation a payment? | No. It says how much you are ready to give. You can change or withdraw it while the Need is open. | Нет. Это заявление, сколько вы готовы дать. Его можно изменить или отозвать, пока потребность открыта. |

### 6. Why believe it

1. **The world is already connected.** Two Facebook users were 4.74 handshakes apart on average in
   2012 and 3.57 in 2016 (Backstrom et al., 2012; Edunov et al., 2016).
2. **It works with real people.** The network grew from one participant to about 300 through real
   acquaintances and handled real Needs (Lubalin's report, July 2026; 287 people with connections on
   13 November 2026).
3. **Nothing sits in the middle.** No money passes through the app, there is no fee and no operator,
   the code is open and the project is non-profit.

### 7. What it is not (for the FAQ and the press, not the first screen)

A cold reader took a list of denials on the first screen as a sign of something regulated, so
these lines belong in the FAQ and in answers to press and regulators.

| EN | RU |
|---|---|
| Not insurance: nobody pays in advance and nobody promises a payout. | Не страховка: никто не платит заранее и никто не обещает выплат. |
| Not a fund: there is no pot and no treasurer. | Не фонд: нет общего котла и казначея. |
| Not donations to strangers: a Need travels only along chains of people who know each other, and next time you may be the one who asks. | Не пожертвования незнакомым: потребность идёт только по цепочкам знакомых людей, и в следующий раз попросить можете вы. |
| Not a pyramid: nobody pays to join and nobody earns from inviting. | Не пирамида: за вход никто не платит и на приглашениях никто не зарабатывает. |

### 8. One line and one ask per audience

| Audience | One line (EN) | One line (RU) | The ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| Future participant | The network has to exist before the bad day. | Сеть нужна до трудного дня, а не в него. | Add the people you know in person. |
| Community leader | Your tradition of mutual support, beyond your own circle. | Ваша традиция взаимной поддержки, за пределами вашего круга. | Try it with your group; we prepare the story in your tradition's words. |
| Builder, researcher | An open social utility for invitational coordination, with a theory and a plan to test it. | Открытая социальная утилита для добровольной координации, с теорией и планом проверки. | Work on the code or the measurements. |
| Donor | Fund the test, not the app: the app and the theory already exist. | Финансируйте проверку, а не приложение: приложение и теория уже есть. | Fund the deployment in communities and the measurement of whether growth sustains itself. |
| Press, regulator | Two signals between people who know each other; no money passes through. | Два сигнала между знакомыми людьми; деньги через приложение не идут. | Read the FAQ and the terms. |

### 9. The words

Always: network of people you know, Need, Participation, risk sharing, nearest first.
Never: pool, fund, insurance, donation, guaranteed. Full table: [vocabulary](../principles/vocabulary.md).

Out of this message: direct cooperation, initiatives, AI agents and mutual offset of obligations
([parked topics](../future/parked-topics.md)). The Law of Autocatalytic Inevitability and its symbols
are for builders and donors only, always with its conditions ([the law](../theory/autocatalytic-law.md)).

### 10. How to check it with people (before stage 3)

Show the headline with its two lines and the five beats to five people who have never seen SO. Ask:
1. What happens when someone reports a $300 Need? (up to 300 people, nearest first; each decides)
2. Does money go through the app? (no)
3. Is anyone guaranteed to get help? (no)
4. Is Participation a payment? (no, a statement of what you are ready to give)
5. Why join now? (the network has to exist before the bad day)

It passes if four of five answer all five in their own words. Write down every reading of it as
insurance, a fund, a pyramid or a donation site.

### 11. Cold read, 2026-11-18

A model with no context read the first draft as a newcomer. It understood that money does not go
through the app, that nobody is guaranteed help and why to join early. What it tripped on, and the
fix in this version:

| It tripped on | It first thought | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "one person for each dollar" | 300 people each give $1 | "up to 300 people for $300" |
| "take a small part" | a part of what? | "a small part of the amount" |
| "report Participation, from $1" | reporting charges me | "say how much they are ready to give… Nobody pays through the app" + FAQ row |
| "what the author did before" | a reputation check, privacy | the chain and "ask the one you know" |
| "Money, if any" | suspicious, no protection | "If they agree on money, they hand it over to each other" |
| "five people… ten in your network", "every person you add" | recruiting, a pyramid | access rule moved to the app; "Nobody earns from inviting" |
| a list of denials | something regulated | moved to the FAQ |
| "share each other's risks" | insurance | kept (agreed wording); tested in section 10 |
| "The world is already connected" | vague marketing | supporting fact added |
| "Tomorrow the Need may be yours" | pressure | "Next time, the one who asks may be you" |

Its remaining question, "Who checks that promised money actually arrives, and does the app take any
cut?", is now answered in section 5.

### 12. What it changes on the live site (stage 3, not now)

- `landing.steps.s3Text` says a Need "reaches exactly {{n}} people" and "closes once participants
  cover the amount". It should say "up to" and "stops taking Participation once the amount is
  reported" (the author closes it).
- The hero takes the headline from section 1; the denials stay in the FAQ.
