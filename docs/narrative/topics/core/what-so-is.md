# What Social Organizer is (and is not)

> Summary: Canonical descriptions of Social Organizer at several lengths (EN and RU), what it is not, and the facts that stay true in every description.

## Context

Every text about SO (landing, video, post, pitch, store listing, assistant answer) should be
derivable from the lines below. They follow the story order agreed with the project's inspirer:
the network comes first, signals travel inside it, coordination is a consequence
([product narrative decisions](../meta/sources.md)). Change these lines only with the team.

## Details

### Facts that stay true in every description

1. People first build a network of people they know in person; each connection is confirmed.
2. A Need names an amount in dollars and reaches as many people as it names dollars, nearest by
   handshakes first.
3. Each notified person decides for themselves; Participation is voluntary, small and changeable
   while the Need is open.
4. Participants talk and agree directly, in a chat in any messenger.
5. Social Organizer does not accept, transfer or hold money, takes no fee, does not judge, and
   guarantees nothing. Open source, non-profit.
6. What happened stays visible as activity history: facts, not ratings.

The two paragraphs below are the earlier versions, kept for reference.

**One paragraph (EN, earlier).** You build a network of people you know in person, and they build theirs.
When one of you has a hard time, you report a Need with an amount and a chat link. It reaches as
many people as it names dollars, nearest by handshakes first, and each of them sees who connects
them to you. They find out what happened in their own way, report Participation if they want to,
often a dollar or a few, and agree on the rest with you directly. Nobody in the middle holds the
money or decides who deserves it. Being part of such a network makes your own risks bearable in
advance, because tomorrow the Need may be yours.

**Один абзац (RU, ранняя версия).** Вы собираете сеть из людей, которых знаете лично, а они собирают свою. Когда
кому-то из вас трудно, вы сообщаете о потребности: сумма и ссылка на чат. Её получают столько
людей, сколько в ней долларов, сначала ближайшие по рукопожатиям, и каждый видит, через кого он
связан с вами. Люди сами выясняют обстоятельства, при желании сообщают об участии (часто это
доллар или несколько) и договариваются с вами напрямую. Никто посередине не держит деньги и не
решает, кто достоин. Участие в такой сети заранее делает ваши собственные риски переносимыми:
завтра потребность может быть вашей.

### What Social Organizer is not

| It is not | Why the difference matters |
|---|---|
| A donation platform for strangers (GoFundMe) | Needs travel only through people who know each other; giving is mutual over time, not one-way. |
| Insurance, a fund or a pool | Nobody pays in advance, nobody promises a payout, there is no common pot. China's mutual aid platforms were shut as unlicensed insurers. |
| A payment service | Money, if any, moves directly between people, outside SO. |
| An arbiter or a rating system | SO records facts; people interpret them. |
| A social feed or an audience | Followers do not count; only confirmed acquaintances do. |
| A charity | The participant protects their own future too. |

### Internal framing (for builders and theory, not for a first contact)

In Lubalin's report (2026) Social Organizer is a **social utility** that implements invitational
coordination: people formalise their existing significant relations by invitation and bilateral
acceptance, the relations combine into a referential graph, and two informational messages (Need,
Participation) start direct interaction that happens outside the utility. Its purpose is to
activate the latent global connectedness that already exists (on average 4.57 handshakes, fewer than
four people in between, among Facebook users in 2016). See [invitational coordination](../theory/invitational-coordination.md) and
[latent connectedness](../theory/latent-connectedness.md). The canonical glossary
(`glossary_social_organizer.md`, Russian) says the same in earlier words ("not a platform, not a
service, not a product"). The Law of Autocatalytic Inevitability is **conditional** in the author's
own text; state it with its thresholds ([the law](../theory/autocatalytic-law.md), [claims](../principles/claims.md)).
