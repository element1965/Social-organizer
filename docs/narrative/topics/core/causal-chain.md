# The causal chain — from risk sharing to a self-developing environment

> Summary: The project's central thesis (brainstorm of 2026-11-17): risk sharing starts the process, direct cooperation is its natural continuation, and the resulting social environment can develop on its own — with what is a fact and what is a hypothesis.

## Context

This is the answer to "why is Social Organizer more than a tool for covering emergencies?". It
came out of the 2026-11-17 brainstorm (Andrei Lubalin's messages, see [sources](../meta/sources.md)).
Use it with builders, researchers and donors. The participant-facing video and landing show only
links 1–2; links 3–5 are the horizon and must be labelled as the next stage.

## Details

### The chain

| # | Link | Status today |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A network of people you know.** People confirm real acquaintances; through them each person reaches the 2nd and 3rd handshake. | Works in the app. |
| 2 | **Protection through mutual risk sharing.** A person takes part in covering others' risks because they want their own life to be predictable. A referential graph, activity history and the habit of direct contact form. | Works in the app (Need and Participation). |
| 3 | **Beyond the first circle.** Looking for a builder, a doctor, a project partner or a flat, a person can find candidates in the graph, see the chain of acquaintances and their activity history. Friendly prices, direct agreements and joint initiatives can spread through one social fabric instead of staying inside separate communities. | Next stage. Partly visible today (profiles, chains); the old skills matching is switched off in the MVP (`mvp-scope.ts`). Not a product promise. |
| 4 | **The risk of cooperation changes.** If a direct deal fails (a €5,000 advance, no work done), the person who lost can report a Need to cover the loss, and the story may become known to people connected to both sides. Two parallel processes: the loss is spread among volunteers; the social consequences become visible. No central arbiter. | Possible today in mechanics (any Need); the framing is a thesis. |
| 5 | **A self-reproducing dynamic.** More cooperation → more history of interactions → more information for the next decisions → more direct cooperation. The environment can then develop independently of the first tool. | Hypothesis to validate with data: the [law](../theory/autocatalytic-law.md) names the thresholds (N*, m(N) > 1) and the parameters to measure. |

### The central thesis, in the author's words

> RU (original): «В среде взаимного перераспределения рисков прямая кооперация становится
> рационально привлекательнее, поскольку её потенциальные материальные последствия могут быть
> распределены между множеством участников, а история взаимодействий становится социально видимой.»
>
> EN: In an environment of mutual risk sharing, direct cooperation becomes rationally more
> attractive, because its possible material consequences can be spread among many participants
> and the history of interactions becomes socially visible.

> RU: «Перераспределение рисков запускает процесс. Прямая кооперация становится его естественным
> продолжением. А возникающая социальная среда получает возможность развиваться независимо от
> первоначального инструмента.»
>
> EN: Risk sharing starts the process. Direct cooperation is its natural continuation. The social
> environment that emerges gets the chance to develop independently of the original tool.

### How it connects to the trust research

Links 2–5 are Case's conditions growing on each other: risk sharing is the win-win (condition 2);
the graph and the history make every interaction part of a repeated game (condition 1); the chat
and the chain keep miscommunication low (condition 3). Link 5 is Case's evolution chapter:
successful behaviour gets imitated, "we are each other's environment". See
[trust conditions in SO](../trust/trust-conditions-in-so.md).

### Precision rules (agreed in the brainstorm)

- The possibility to cover a loss is not a guarantee of compensation.
- Reputational consequences do not exclude repeat fraud.
- SO does not judge, does not establish truth, does not assign a reliability score and does not
  impose sanctions. Reputation is a result of social relationships, not a rating from the platform.
- Excluding significant losses entirely would require proving that participation is always
  sufficient. We do not claim that.
