# Idea — a "How It Works" video (from the brainstorm, not started)

> Summary: The brainstorm's proposal for a 2–3 minute practical video complementing the published "Direct Mutual Support Through Real Connections" — its plot, principle and scope — recorded as an idea; no task has been given.

## Context

Proposed in the 2026-11-17 brainstorm as a separate working project, "Social Organizer — How It
Works". **Status: idea.** The user has not set it as a task (2026-11-18). It is kept here because
its plot is the clearest statement so far of how people use the network, and the landing and
in-app teaching can reuse it.

## Details

- **Purpose:** a practical 2–3 minute video, complementing the published *Direct Mutual Support
  Through Real Connections*, showing how real people use SO for their own protection through
  mutual risk sharing. Real screens of the working app plus scenes of live communication outside it.
- **Plot:** the seven steps of the [participant's journey](../mechanics/user-journey.md) (graph →
  notification → own inquiry → crowdfunding of intentions → live interaction → closing and activity
  history → own protection), plus a short part on regular support by analogy with Patreon.
- **Main principle:** show what people become able to do themselves thanks to the app, not what
  the app does instead of them.
- **Scope:** no initiatives, AI agents or mutual offset of obligations ([parked](../future/parked-topics.md)).
- **Uses:** landing, YouTube, distribution through influencers.

### Facts already checked, if the idea is taken up

The brief's wording needs three corrections against the live app (details in the journey note):
the app counts in **US dollars** ($1, not €1); the author's activity history is **one tap away** on
their page, not on the Need screen; say **activity history**, not reputation history. The old
Playwright recording scripts in `docs/first-steps/` and `docs/regular-collection/` no longer
match the current screens; their sign-in and recording code can be reused.
