# Landing concepts from the brainstorm (not decided)

> Summary: Two brainstorm proposals for the new landing — "The World Is Already Connected" (five scenes around the real 3D graph in a demo mode) and the "one life situation, three moving steps" first screen from the landing-references chat — with the corrections they need against the live app and our rules.

## Context

Input for stage 3 of the [packaging plan](packaging-plan.md). Sources: the second part of the chat
"Финансирование социальной инновации" (`6afce8ee…`) and the chat "Аналоги лендингов" (`6af7620c…`),
both read 2026-11-18. These are ChatGPT's proposals inside those chats; the user had not looked at
them as concepts yet (2026-11-18), so show them before asking for a choice. The author's brief in both: the current landing is "one text"; it should be
dynamic, simple, possibly with a person telling the story; the graph prototype on the page is worth
keeping. **Status: ideas, nothing chosen.**

## Details

### Concept A — "The World Is Already Connected"

Principle: the network is the hero of the page, not a picture next to text. The page becomes a
short visual story in five scenes, driven by the existing 3D graph component in a **demo mode**
(fictional people, prepared connections, controlled animations, no sign-in, no server calls). The
same scenes serve both the interactive page and a video.

Hero: *The world is already connected. Now we can act together.* Sub: *Discover what becomes
possible when real human connections form a network.* Buttons: *Explore the network* / *Watch how
it works*. Line: *Open source · Non-profit · Voluntary participation*.

| # | Scene | What happens | Caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | We already know each other | One person appears, then five acquaintances; each confirms; they invite theirs | *You know someone. They know someone else. And so on.* |
| 02 | A network of real connections | The camera pulls back to rings 1–3; selecting a person lights the path through shared acquaintances | *Every connection has a human reference.* |
| 03 | One person has a need | A participant reports an unexpected expense; the signal spreads through the graph; people may report Participation | *A need becomes visible. Participation is voluntary.* |
| 04 | A little from many | Many small voluntary shares cover one large Need; people interact directly | *Little from many. Enough for one.* |
| 05 | And the network keeps growing | More people; the history of confirmed actions; later, finding collaborators and joint initiatives | *From mutual risk sharing to new possibilities for cooperation.* |

Below it, only three short sections: **What can you do today?** (Emergency, Regular), **What becomes
possible next?** (initiatives, collaborators: a future stage), **Start with people you know.**
FAQ, legal notes, GitHub, the research and the TEDx talk stay, off the main path. A ~2-minute
video draft has the author on camera at the start and the end (see the
[video idea](how-it-works-video-idea.md)).

Fit with the theory: scene 01 is the report's empirical premise (the world is already connected,
fewer than four people between two Facebook users in 2016) turned into a headline.

### Concept B — "one situation, three moving steps" (landing-references chat)

- Open with one life situation instead of a description of the system: *when one person needs a
  large sum, it can be spread among many people connected to them.*
- Then three steps with motion: people connect through acquaintances → someone reports a Need →
  others report Participation and coordinate directly.
- An arithmetic example of "little from many", with an on-screen note that the app holds and moves
  no money and people confirm participation themselves.
- A 45–60 second video: short voice-over, real app screens, simple animation of one Need spreading;
  a face at the start and the end; subtitles; opens on tap; a silent looping scene can play inline.
- First screen: one strong statement, the video or animated example, two buttons (*See how it
  works*, *Download the app*).
- Conclusion of the chat: explain SO with one scene — one Need → a network of real connections →
  voluntary participation of many → direct interaction between people.

Reference landings from that chat are listed in [comparables](../audiences/comparables.md).

### Corrections both concepts need

| In the concept | Correction |
|---|---|
| €300, "30 × €10" | The app counts in US dollars. And a $300 Need **reaches 300 people**, nearest first; "30 people × $10" is fine as an illustration of how Participation adds up, if the reach rule is stated correctly. |
| "people connected to them directly" | Through handshakes: the 1st ring first, then the 2nd and 3rd. |
| "The history of confirmed actions creates the basis of reputation" | Say "activity history"; reputation forms between people, the app gives no score. |
| "Social Organizer helps to report a Need…" | People act; the app is the channel (`so-story`). |
| "What becomes possible next: initiatives" on the landing | A parked topic: decide whether a clearly marked future section belongs on a participant-facing page ([open questions](../meta/open-questions.md)). |
| "Initiatives — crowdfunding of intentions" | Correct as is: the term covers Participation in a Need today and initiatives later (user, 2026-11-18). |

### What both agree on

The network is shown, not described; real app components rather than stock pictures; minimal text;
one scene that a newcomer can follow; video as a companion to a clear headline, not a replacement.
This matches the `so-explainer` method (act, predict, see).
