# Audiences and what each needs to hear

> Summary: The five audiences of the packaging work (future participants, community leaders and influencers, like-minded builders, donors, press and regulators), their main question, the answer, and which material serves each.

## Context

The packaging work (video, landing, in-app teaching, pitch) serves several audiences at once. They
share the same facts ([what SO is](../core/what-so-is.md)) but enter at different points of the
[causal chain](../core/causal-chain.md). Traction numbers must come from production data with a
date (on 2026-11-13: 287 people with connections; the cold-start hypothesis puts the threshold of
self-sustaining growth at 10–15 thousand, a hypothesis).

## Details

| Audience | Their main question | The answer in one line | Enters the chain at | Material |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Future participant** (got a link from someone they know) | "Why join now, when I don't need anything?" | Your own risks become bearable before the bad day comes, and it starts with five people you know. | Links 1–2 | How It Works video, landing, invite page, in-app intro |
| **Community leader / influencer** (carries a mutual-aid tradition: harambee and chama in Kenya, stokvel in South Africa, tandas in Mexico, vaquinha and mutirão in Brazil) | "How is this different from what my community already does?" | The same spirit, without a treasurer, a pot or a size limit: each person confirms whom they know, and a Need reaches the nearest first. | Links 1–2 | Influencer kit: video, 30-second version, talking points, a QR/link to their own network |
| **Like-minded builder** (developer, designer, researcher) | "What is the idea behind it, and can I take part?" | An open-source tool that changes the game so that trust between people who know each other becomes rational. | Links 1–5 | Causal chain, trust research, README, open issues |
| **Donor / funder** | "What exactly would my money do, and where does it go?" | It funds development, translations, legal review and research into whether the growth thesis holds. No money of participants ever passes through the project. | Links 1–5 | One-page pitch, the causal chain, current data with dates, the project's own Regular Needs (author and developer profiles) |
| **Press, store reviewers, regulators** | "Is this a financial service or insurance?" | No: no payments, no pot, no payout promises, no fee. People send two signals and act directly. | Link 2 | FAQ, store texts, terms |

### Notes per audience

- **Participant.** Lead with a situation they recognise (Splitwise's lesson), then the network,
  then the Need. Do not start with money or with "coordination".
- **Influencer.** Hui/ROSCA rotation is not our mechanism; say "same spirit, different mechanics".
  Each influencer brings a whole sub-network, so the invite path (link → understand → join →
  confirm) must work in one minute.
- **Builder.** They will check the claims. Give them the precision rules and the open questions.
- **Donor.** Funding the project works the way the project works: the author and developer
  profiles have open Regular Needs (SPEC §6). Name what is a fact and what is a hypothesis.
- **Regulator.** Use the exact store wording ("does not accept, transfer or hold funds and does
  not guarantee an outcome").
