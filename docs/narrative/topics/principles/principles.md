# Principles of Social Organizer

> Summary: The principles every SO text, screen and feature must respect — the network first, people act and the app enables, voluntary and independent decisions, direct connection, no arbiter or rating, payments outside, possibility is not a guarantee.

## Context

Collected from the landing ("Principles"), SPEC §5 "What the system does not have", the store
texts in `docs/release/launch-readiness.md`, the inspirer's critique of 2026-11-11 and the
brainstorm of 2026-11-17. When a new idea conflicts with one of these, the idea changes, or the
team changes the principle explicitly.

## Details

1. **The network comes first.** People build a network of people they know in person, and signals
   travel only through confirmed acquaintances. Any story starts here, not with money or coordination.
2. **People act; the app enables.** Show what people become able to do themselves thanks to the
   app, not what the app does instead of them. Test: in each sentence the subject is a person.
3. **Voluntary participation (invitational).** Nobody has to respond. Declining changes nothing for
   the person: refusal carries no consequence imposed by anyone (Lubalin's test of an invitational
   interaction).
4. **Independent decision.** Each person finds out the circumstances in their own way and decides.
   No organiser decides for anyone.
5. **Direct connection.** Participants agree and act among themselves, in their own chat in any
   messenger, with nobody in between.
6. **No arbiter, no rating.** SO records facts and does not judge, establish truth, score
   reliability or impose sanctions. Reputation is a result of social relationships, not a
   platform's mark. The ignore list is private and reversible.
7. **Money stays outside.** SO does not accept, transfer or hold funds and takes no fee. Payments
   and the execution of agreements happen outside the Organizer.
8. **Risk sharing, not a pool.** Nobody pays in advance, nobody promises a payout, there is no
   common pot and no operator.
9. **Possibility is not a guarantee.** A Need can be covered; it may not be. A loss can be shared;
   compensation is not guaranteed. Visibility makes deception costly, not impossible.
10. **One signal, no pressure.** A Need notifies each person once, nearest first, as many people as
    it names dollars. No reminder spam, no scheduled messages that people did not ask for.
11. **Open and non-profit.** The code is open; the project is non-profit; one shared network.
12. **Informational only.** Every operation in the app is a message: it moves no resources, creates
    no obligation and assigns no executor ("zero direct operational harm").
13. **Entry through a real relation.** Connections are formalised by invitation and bilateral
    acceptance, and the path of entry stays visible (referential inheritance).
14. **No centre.** The process lives in direct interactions between people; there is no
    organisational centre that could be captured, prohibited or destroyed.
