# Parked topics — not part of the current story

> Summary: Topics deliberately kept out of the current video, landing and onboarding (mutual offset of obligations, initiatives, AI agents, non-monetary Needs) and why.

## Context

The 2026-11-17 brainstorm: "we will not mix this with initiatives, AI agents and mutual offset of
obligations; these are later stages of the environment's development". Mixing them in now would
blur the first, simplest story and make claims the app cannot back yet.

## Details

| Topic | What it is | Why parked | Where it may appear |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mutual offset of obligations** (взаимозачёт обязательств) | A future economic architecture where obligations between participants can be netted | A separate theme; "we will not go into the future economic architecture now" | Builder and research materials, labelled as future |
| **Initiatives** | Joint projects of participants growing out of the network | Later stage of the environment | Causal chain, link 3+ |
| **AI agents** | Agents acting for participants in the environment | Later stage; also conflicts with "people act" unless framed carefully | Not in public materials yet |
| **Direct cooperation** | Finding partners in the graph, friendly prices | Next stage after risk sharing | [Direct cooperation](direct-cooperation.md), builder/donor materials |
| **Non-monetary Needs** | A Need expressed other than in dollars | Open product question after the release (launch-readiness §3) | — |

Rule: a participant-facing piece (video, landing, onboarding, invite) does not mention these.
A builder or donor piece may, in a clearly marked "what comes next" section.
