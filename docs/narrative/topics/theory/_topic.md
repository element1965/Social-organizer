# Theory

The project's own theory (Lubalin): invitational coordination, latent connectedness, the conditional law of self-sustaining growth.

- [The Law of Autocatalytic Inevitability — thresholds, measurement, limits](autocatalytic-law.md) — Lubalin's formal law of self-sustaining activation (k, q(N), p(N), m(N) > 1, critical mass N*), why "inevitability" is conditional, the parameters a deployment must measure, the conditions that would disconfirm it, and what the MVP did and did not test.
- [Invitational coordination — the theory behind SO (Lubalin, 2026)](invitational-coordination.md) — Andrei Lubalin's core distinction — coercive vs invitational interaction by the consequence of refusal, the two coordination mechanisms (command → control → coercion; idea → consensus → assistance) and the causal sequence from mechanism to social environment — and how it meets Case's "the game defines the players".
- [Latent global connectedness and the conditions of its activation](latent-connectedness.md) — The empirical premise (the world's social graph is already connected — 4.74 and 3.57 degrees in Facebook data), the motivation (a calculable benefit of spreading one person's loss among many), the four conditions of activation, and what Social Organizer does and does not do as a "social utility".
